# M2 scope v2 (owner decisions V6-V15, 25/10/26)

This is what was built after Sahil widened version 1 on 25/10/26, and the honest status of each part. "Tested" means it ran for real on the owner's rig. "Mapped" means it is implemented and unit-tested against recorded shapes or fixtures, but has not yet made a real result.

| Area | What a user can do | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Story (V6-V8) | Plain-English story first: synopsis → scene-by-scene script in character voices → editor read → one rewrite. Truth is checked in meaning: names, numbers and who-did-what stay exact, nothing invented. "Behind the panels" notes keep the real technical detail. | Tested: real chapter plan `build-evidence/M2/qa-fix/plan-v2` (after Quan's review and the takeover follow-up). ctx.llm safety eval, meaning mode, two runs after the fixes: 52/52 inventions refused in both; plain-English rewrites accepted 6/8 and 7/8 (Quan measured 7/8 and 7/8 before the fixes, not the 8/8 first reported). Counts and paths: `docs/m2-status.json`. Limit: Quan found misattributed lines and wrong source ids in the earlier plan-4 evidence (QAR-M2-01); a takeover spot-check found the same on the first post-fix plan (`qa-fix/plan`), because statements with no claim token still cited the whole panel. Now every statement cites, and is judged against, only the events that state it. Re-review is pending. |
| Long chapters (V4/V5) | 3-4 page chapters (16-24 panels) with wordless filler panels (about 1 per 2 story panels), comic-book page layout and `chapter.pdf`. The short strip is one page (6 story + 3 fillers). | Tested: 18-panel, 4-page chapter rendered (`render-chapter1-anime`). |
| Image sources (V10) | `hermes reel sources` lists them, `--check` verifies one at no cost, and `--set` saves the default. Render takes `--source/--model/--workflow`. | Host (Hermes `image_generate`): tested. OpenAI, Gemini (Nano Banana), fal (FLUX, Nano Banana): mapped. On 25/10/26 each live request reached the provider and stopped at billing (no credit). ComfyUI (Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 workflow): tested. One owner-approved local panel was made with a reference sheet: 1536×1024 in 308 s, rated 4/5. Faint unreadable sign lettering appeared in the background. |
| Styles (V14) | 13 built-in styles, 10 of them raw prompt files (`styles/<key>/style.md`), including the owner's ukiyo-e folktale. Users drop in their own `style.md`. Any style works with any character: another style's approved sheet fixes identity. Choose with `--style` or `hermes reel styles --set`. | anime and ink-pen-sketch: tested. The other 11 are experimental (not yet rendered). Render warns; `--strict-styles` refuses them. |
| Characters (V11) | `kit import-multiverse` imports the 32 Hermes Multiverse characters with published sheets (checksum-verified; full resolution from the release ZIP, or 960px previews). `kit create` runs a set-up exercise; photo likeness is allowed only for yourself. `kit contribute` builds a Multiverse-shaped bundle and PR text, and pushes nothing. Kit voices feed the writer. | Import: tested on the real Multiverse checkout. After rights enforcement (QAR-M2-03), 31 characters were imported with 280 preview sheets. 3 were skipped: emozilla and ggb (documentation only) and sudo-nightwing (creator approval pending). Create and contribute: unit-tested; no real sheet generated yet. |
| Harnesses (V12) | `hermes reel plan --harness claude-code,codex,…` reads other agents' local logs read-only, alone or alongside Hermes. A generic JSONL mapping covers Command Code and others. | Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode: tested read-only on local logs (text round-trips). Pi and generic JSONL: mapped (fixtures; no local sessions exist). |
| Monthly book (V13) | `hermes reel book --month YYYY-MM` builds a cover, contents and every episode's pages into one PDF. A monthly blueprint runs it on the 1st. | Tested: a real 8-page book from three existing renders. |
| Schedule (item 4) | Daily strip, weekly strip or chapter, and monthly book blueprints, each with time, format and style slots. Nothing runs until the user creates an automation. | Tested in live Hermes: all three register and fill to valid schedules (`30 18 * * *`, `30 18 * * 5`, `0 9 1 * *`). No automation created. |
