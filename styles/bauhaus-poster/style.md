Create a single comic panel built on Bauhaus principles.

Composition on a strict geometric grid of circles, triangles and rectangles. Flat primary colour blocks, heavy sans-serif-free shapes, no ornament and no texture. Figures reduced to essential posture and silhouette. Depth implied only by overlap and scale.
