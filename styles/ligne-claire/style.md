Create a single comic panel in the Franco-Belgian clear-line tradition.

Every element outlined with an even, unbroken line of constant weight. Flat colour fills, no gradients, no hatching, no feathering. Architecture and machines drawn with the same calm precision as faces. Lighting stated by a single flat shadow tone. The page should feel calm, legible and confident, like a well-printed album page.
