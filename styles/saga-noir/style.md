The purpose of this prompt is **not** to define the image itself, but to define the artistic identity, atmosphere, craftsmanship, and visual language. The actual image, story, composition, symbolism, and narrative should always be driven by the supplied inputs below.

# The Saga Noir Studio

Every illustration should feel like a full-page spread from a legendary graphic novel published by Saga Noir Studio.

Regardless of subject matter, every artwork should appear to belong to a single coherent collection of museum-quality graphic illustrations.

The viewer should immediately recognise the artist without predicting the image.

---

# Artistic Philosophy

Shapes before details.

Every object reinforces story.

Every shadow strengthens composition.

Remove visual clutter. Only meaningful information remains.

Never create photorealistic, CGI, 3D rendered, painterly realism, or generic fantasy art.

Never create generic AI art.

Instead, create illustrations that feel like they were produced by a master graphic novelist using traditional media — bold, expressive, imperfect, powerful.

The artwork should be recognisable from across a room.

---

# Style

Blend together influences from:

- Nordic mythology
- European graphic novels
- American prestige comics
- heavy ink illustration
- woodcut printmaking
- dramatic poster art
- cinematic concept art
- heavy metal album art
- fantasy realism

The final result should feel entirely hand crafted.

Never photorealistic.

Never CGI.

Never 3D rendered.

Never painterly realism.

Never generic fantasy.

Never glossy.

---

# Graphic Language

Shapes before details.

Use:

- enormous silhouettes
- bold shadow masses
- iconic poses
- simplified forms
- dramatic negative space
- powerful framing
- graphic abstraction
- strong visual hierarchy

The artwork should be recognisable from across a room.

Every shape should read instantly.

Details are earned, not assumed.

---

# Ink Work

Use:

- heavy black shadows
- bold contour lines
- expressive line weight
- rough dry-brush
- cross-hatching
- scratched highlights
- visible ink grain
- energetic brush marks

Allow roughness.

Allow imperfections.

Imperfection creates authenticity.

Ink should feel physical — applied with force and intention.

---

# Colour Palette

Restrict to 3-4 dominant colours from:

- deep forest green
- oxidised teal
- muted turquoise
- charcoal
- ivory
- warm black
- aged cream

Accent colours:

- crimson
- blood red
- burnt orange
- ochre
- pale gold
- lightning white

No rainbow palettes.

No bright saturation.

Colours should feel printed — like offset lithography or screenprint.

---

# Lighting

Use mythological light sources:

- lightning
- eclipses
- full moons
- glowing horizons
- firelight
- northern lights
- divine beams
- cosmic storms

Light carves silhouettes.

Darkness is part of the composition.

Lighting should create emotional scale rather than technical accuracy.

---

# Composition

Compose every illustration like a legendary comic splash page.

Use:

- dominant foreground hero
- secondary symbolic element
- vast negative space
- dramatic sky

Perspective techniques:

- low angle
- worm's-eye view
- extreme perspective
- heroic framing
- monumental scale

The artwork should remain visually powerful even when viewed as a small thumbnail.

Larger viewing should continuously reward exploration with additional details.

---

# Human Presence

Mythic characters.

Restrained expressions.

Emotion conveyed through:

- posture
- silhouette
- stance
- lighting
- composition

No exaggerated facial acting.

Quiet strength.

Figures should feel like legendary archetypes — gods, heroes, guardians, wanderers — not realistic people.

---

# Environment

Landscapes as characters.

Use:

- towering cliffs
- ancient forests
- storm seas
- ruined temples
- frozen mountains
- colossal architecture
- impossible skies

Environments should feel monumental rather than detailed.

The landscape should tell its own story within the composition.

---

# Storytelling

Translate every subject into mythology.

Never illustrate literally.

Examples:

- AI → forging a divine weapon
- Cybersecurity → guardians at a sacred gate
- Finance → kings protecting an ancient vault
- Open-source → clans forging weapons together
- Distributed systems → longships sailing separately across a storm sea
- Climate change → Yggdrasil struggling to hold the world together
- Business competition → rival jarls facing each other across a misty field
- Scientific discovery → a forgotten rune being deciphered by torchlight

Invent fresh symbolism appropriate to the supplied Concept.

Avoid repeating the same metaphors across multiple artworks.

---

# Texture

Use analogue media characteristics:

- visible paper grain
- ink absorption
- dry brush texture
- rough printing marks
- screenprint texture
- scratched pigment
- distressed edges
- subtle halftones

The artwork should feel physically printed.

The viewer should feel the paper and ink when looking at the image.

---

# Information Density

Only meaningful information.

Remove visual clutter.

Every object reinforces the story.

Every shadow strengthens the composition.

Do not fill every space with detail.

Allow negative space to carry meaning and power.

---

# Atmosphere

Every illustration should evoke:

- power
- legend
- destiny
- courage
- sacrifice
- ancient wisdom
- mystery
- awe
- timelessness
- mythic scale

The emotional atmosphere should remain consistent regardless of subject matter.

---

# Flexibility

This prompt defines only the visual identity.

It must **never** dictate the actual content of the illustration.

The supplied **Concept** determines what the artwork depicts.

The optional **Reference** influences composition, atmosphere and mood without ever being copied.

The optional **Mood** adjusts emotional presentation while preserving the style.

No recurring imagery, characters, architecture, or visual metaphors should become permanently associated with this style.

Every illustration should feel completely original while remaining unmistakably part of the Saga Noir Studio collection.

The viewer should recognise the **artist** — not predict the **image**.

---

# Final Objective

Create an extraordinary graphic illustration that feels like a full-page spread from **Saga Noir Studio** — a legendary graphic novel publishing house known for bold silhouettes, mythological storytelling, dramatic composition, restrained colour, expressive ink, cinematic perspective, and graphic power.

The finished artwork should possess a timeless artistic identity that remains instantly recognisable across an unlimited variety of completely different subjects, while never becoming repetitive.

The consistency should come from the bold silhouettes, mythological storytelling, dramatic composition, restrained colour, expressive ink, cinematic perspective, and graphic power — not from reusing the same imagery.
