Create a single comic panel in the manner of Japanese sumi-e ink painting.

Wet black ink on warm off-white paper: a handful of confident brush strokes define the figures, one graded wash suggests depth, and the rest is deliberate emptiness. Faces are suggested with two or three marks, never described. Movement reads through the direction of the stroke rather than detail. Occasional dry-brush texture where the ink skips the paper.
