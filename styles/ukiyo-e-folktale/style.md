**ARTISTIC STYLE**

Fuse traditional Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock printmaking with European Art Nouveau, antique botanical engravings, psychedelic 1970s fantasy illustration, and elaborate hand-printed folk-art tapestries.

The illustration should feel like a rediscovered masterpiece from an alternative-history fantasy civilization — something ancient, mystical, sophisticated and slightly surreal.

**VISUAL TECHNIQUES**

- Extremely intricate, fine engraved linework and delicate crosshatching.
- Organic, flowing contours that interweave characters, creatures, vegetation and architecture.
- Flattened perspective mixed with carefully layered cinematic depth.
- Richly textured, weathered handmade paper with visible print grain, ink imperfections and aged pigmentation.
- Elaborate decorative motifs inspired by Japanese textiles, traditional woodblock prints and illuminated manuscripts.
- Intricate botanical patterns with individually illustrated leaves, twisting roots, flowers, vines and mushrooms.
- Bold silhouettes balanced against densely ornamented backgrounds.
- Subtle asymmetry within an otherwise harmonious, carefully arranged composition.
- Hand-drawn character detailing with elegant facial features, expressive eyes and elaborate costume patterns.

**COLOUR PALETTE**

Use an unconventional yet harmonious combination of faded vermilion, dusty coral, muted jade, deep petrol blue, antique ivory, burnt ochre, moss green, charcoal black and tarnished gold.

Introduce selective accents of luminous turquoise, pale cyan and soft amber to create supernatural energy.

Avoid modern neon gradients and generic fantasy lighting. Colours should feel like multiple layers of hand-applied inks on antique Japanese washi paper.

**ENVIRONMENT**

Build an imaginative, dreamlike world where natural architecture and impossible geometry coexist.

Ancient trees twist into spiralling gateways. Oversized mushrooms become architectural structures. Floating islands, delicate bridges and distant mythical buildings emerge through layered forests. Unusual woodland creatures blend into decorative floral arrangements.

Use hidden miniature details and visual discoveries throughout the environment, rewarding close examination.

**COMPOSITION**

Create a cinematic widescreen illustration with a strong central narrative.

Position the primary characters prominently in the middle ground, framed by elaborate vegetation and organic architectural structures.

Use foreground botanical elements to create depth, while distant structures gradually dissolve into intricate patterned silhouettes.

Every section should contain intentional visual storytelling without becoming cluttered.

**CHARACTER DESIGN**

Characters should combine stylised Japanese fantasy character design with the ornate detailing of historical woodblock illustrations.

Clothing features layered fabrics, engraved armour, intricate embroidery, flowing ribbons, metallic ornaments and beautifully patterned textiles.

Faces should be expressive and attractive but not overly polished or generically anime.

**OVERALL ATMOSPHERE**

Mysterious, enchanting, slightly sinister, whimsical and mythological.

The result should resemble a lavish illustrated folktale that somehow captures the scale and drama of a cinematic fantasy epic.

**AVOID**

Generic anime concept art, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, conventional digital painting, obvious franchise references, excessive bloom, oversaturated neon colours, sterile symmetrical compositions, random visual clutter, generic medieval fantasy armour and obvious AI-generated textures.

**FORMAT**

Landscape 3:2 or cinematic 16:9 composition. Ultra-detailed, high-resolution illustration with carefully controlled visual hierarchy and exceptional craftsmanship. No text, captions, logos or watermarks.
